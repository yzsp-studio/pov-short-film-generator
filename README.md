## 🇨🇳 中文

把一句话创意、剧本、参考图或既有片段转成严格第一人称视角的沉浸式短片生产方案。全片摄影机等于主角眼睛，主角正脸零曝光，可见范围仅限手、鞋、衣袖、胸前物件边缘、身体局部倒影、屏幕反光、呼吸雾气或手持物，任何分镜都必须回答「这一镜头是否可能由主角眼睛看到」，不能就改写。流程包括提炼 POV 核心、选择视觉风格引擎（现实生活纪实、赛博迷幻雨夜霓虹、Y2K 旧录像复古、工业冷冽、电影级质感或自定义）、产出 Final_Video_Spec 规格书、锁定参考图职责（R 风格/C 角色/P 道具/E 场景）、设计 POV 资产与分镜表，并在每条镜头生成前跑反抽卡预检。输出规格书、分镜表、图片与视频提示词、QC 清单和组装建议，关键节点弹确认卡片减少反复「继续」。适合悬疑、生存、恋爱、恐怖、职场与现实题材的 POV 短片或开篇，不用于第三人称剧情、多机位群像或纯产品展示。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 全局硬锁：摄影机=主角眼睛，主角正脸零曝光，每条分镜校验主观可行性
- 6 种视觉风格引擎，自定义感性词必须翻译成光线、色温、噪点、材质等物理锚点
- Final_Video_Spec 规格书：比例、时长、风格映射参数与声音 owner 一次性锁定
- 参考图职责隔离：R 风格 / C 角色 / P 道具 / E 场景，防止风格污染
- POV 分镜表：视点、主角可见局部、外部对象、头部运动、声音证据、禁止项
- 反抽卡预检 Gate + 阶段确认卡片，交付规格书、分镜、提示词与 QC 清单

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a one-line idea, script, reference image or existing clip into a production plan for a strictly first-person immersive short film. The camera is the protagonist's eyes throughout, the protagonist's face is never shown, and what may be visible is limited to hands, shoes, sleeves, the edge of a chest item, partial body reflections, screen glare, breath in cold air or a held object; every shot must answer whether the protagonist's eyes could plausibly see it, or be rewritten. The workflow distils the POV core, picks a visual style engine (real-life documentary, cyber psychedelic neon rain, Y2K camcorder retro, industrial cold, cinematic texture, or a custom physical mapping), produces a Final_Video_Spec sheet, assigns reference-image roles (R style, C character, P prop, E scene), designs POV assets and a shot table, and runs an anti-lottery pre-check before each shot is generated. It outputs the spec sheet, shot table, image and video prompts, a QC checklist and assembly guidance, with confirmation cards at key gates to cut repeated 'continue' typing. Suited to POV shorts or openings in suspense, survival, romance, horror, workplace and slice-of-life genres, not to third-person drama, multi-camera ensembles or pure product showcases.

### ✨ What it does

- Global hard locks: camera equals the protagonist's eyes, zero face exposure, and every shot validated for subjective plausibility
- Six visual style engines, with abstract mood words translated into physical anchors such as light direction, colour temperature, grain and material
- A Final_Video_Spec sheet locking ratio, duration, style-mapping parameters and the sole sound owner in one pass
- Reference-image role isolation — R style, C character, P prop, E scene — to prevent style contamination
- A POV shot table covering viewpoint, visible body parts, external objects, head movement, sound evidence and forbidden items
- An anti-lottery pre-check gate plus stage confirmation cards, delivering the spec, shot table, prompts and QC checklist
